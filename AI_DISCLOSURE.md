# AI assistance and copyright disclosure policy

`oaioauthc` accepts AI-assisted contributions only when a human contributor is
the responsible author of the submitted work. This policy protects the project
and its contributors from unclear authorship and copyright provenance.

## Disclosure

Disclose substantive use of an AI system in the pull request, patch cover
letter, or issue that proposes the change. Identify the affected parts of the
submission and describe the human work that shaped, reviewed, or materially
rewrote them. Do not include private prompts, credentials, or other sensitive
data in the disclosure.

## Human authorship requirement

Purely AI-generated contributions are not accepted. A contributor using AI
assistance must make meaningful human contributions to the submitted result,
including technical judgment and review. The contributor must understand the
change well enough to explain and maintain it.

## Copyright and provenance

By submitting an AI-assisted contribution, the contributor represents that they
have the right to submit it under this project's licensing terms. Contributors
must not knowingly submit material copied from proprietary, confidential, or
incompatibly licensed sources. AI output does not remove the contributor's
responsibility to check provenance and licensing.

## Maintainer review

Maintainers may request clarification about disclosed AI assistance or reject a
submission when human authorship, provenance, licensing, or maintenance
responsibility is unclear. Failure to disclose substantive AI use may result in
rejection of the contribution.
